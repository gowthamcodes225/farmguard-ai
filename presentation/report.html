1. Introduction

FarmGuard AI is a full-stack smart agriculture web platform that uses AI and ML to help farmers detect crop diseases early and receive treatment recommendations. Designed for small farmers in India who lack access to agricultural experts.

2. Problem Statement

Small farmers lose 20–40% of crops annually due to late disease detection. Rural areas lack agricultural experts. Traditional diagnosis is slow, costly, and reactive.

3. Objectives

  • Instant crop disease detection via photo upload
  • AI confidence scores and severity classification
  • Practical treatment recommendations
  • Farmer dashboard with scan history
  • No expensive hardware required

4. Proposed Solution

Web platform: Login → Select crop → Upload leaf photo → AI analysis → Disease + treatment → Dashboard tracking.
