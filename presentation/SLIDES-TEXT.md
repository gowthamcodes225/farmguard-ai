1. Go to folder: `farmguard-ai/presentation/`
2. Double-click `index.html` (opens in Chrome/Edge)
3. Press **F11** for fullscreen
4. Use **Arrow keys** (← →) or **click left/right** to navigate

## Upload to Google Drive (for form)

### Option A — PDF
1. Open `index.html` in Chrome
2. Press Ctrl+P → Destination: **Save as PDF**
3. Upload PDF to Google Drive
4. Share → Anyone with link → Copy link

### Option B — PPT via Google Slides
1. Open https://slides.google.com
2. Create new presentation
3. Copy content from each slide section below

### Slide 2 — Problem
**The Problem**
• 20-40% annual crop loss in India due to plant diseases
• 70% of farmers are small/marginal — limited expert access
• Traditional diagnosis takes days and is expensive
• Late detection = financial loss + food waste

### Slide 3 — Solution
**Our Solution**
FarmGuard AI — Full-stack AI web platform
Upload Photo → AI Analysis → Disease + Treatment → Dashboard
No expensive hardware — just phone + internet

### Slide 4 — Features
**Key Features**
• Secure farmer login
• AI disease scanner (photo upload)
• Confidence score + severity level
• Treatment recommendations
• Analytics dashboard with history
• Mobile-friendly design
