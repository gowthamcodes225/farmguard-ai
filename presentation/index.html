Our Solution — FarmGuard AI

A full-stack AI web platform that lets farmers photograph a crop leaf and get instant disease diagnosis with treatment advice.

How It Works

User Flow

  • Farmer logs into FarmGuard AI
  • Selects crop type (Tomato, Rice, etc.)
  • Uploads photo of affected leaf
  • AI model analyzes the image
  • Results: disease, confidence, severity
  • Treatment recommendation displayed
  • History saved on dashboard
